Daily Final Summary (at bottom of CSV):

  • Total Unique Users: Distinct count of customer accounts.
  • Total Bookings: Distinct count of reservations.
  • Total Tickets Booked: Total individual seats/tickets itemized.
  • Total Base Ticket Amount: Sum of ticket face values.
  • Total Extra Charges: Applicable convenience fees and GST taxes.
  • Total Discounts: Applicable promo code reductions.
  • Total Payable Amount: Grand total billed according to pricing rules.
  • Total Actual Amount Paid: Successfully collected payments.
  • Total Pending Amount: Outstanding balances.
  • Total Refunded Amount: Amounts returned for cancellations.
Financial Safety & Injection Mitigation: Multi-seat bookings distribute convenience fees and tax proportionately across tickets without multiplying booking charges. All cells beginning with formulas (=, +, -, @) are sanitized.
